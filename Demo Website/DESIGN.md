## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a rigorous, technical editorial aesthetic tailored for cutting-edge autonomous intelligence, AI agent laboratories, and high-performance computing platforms. It merges the austere precision of scientific research publications with the vibrant urgency of next-generation developer tooling.

The visual style is **Technical Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Tone & Voice:** Authoritative, razor-sharp, disciplined, and forward-looking.
- **Visual Tension:** Pure architectural whitespace counterbalanced by ink-dense, high-contrast charcoal typography and high-voltage acid lime / kinetic yellow-green accents.
- **Physical Feel:** Tactile paper-white substrates layered beneath monospaced technical telemetry, precise micro-hairlines, and selective bursts of fluorescence. It delivers immediate clarity without decorative bloat.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around severe tonal contrast and surgical chromatic signaling.

- **Primary (`#07110E`):** Deep ink charcoal-black. Anchors primary structural frames, active CTA buttons, and dominant typographic hierarchy.
- **Secondary (`#BAF534`):** High-luminescence electric lime. Reserved strictly for intentional kinetic highlights, active states, execution tags, and system pulses.
- **Tertiary (`#84CC16`):** Focused deep lime for borders, hover surfaces, and visual balance alongside bright lime indicators.
- **Neutral (`#F4F1EA`):** Warm parchment neutral acting as the atmospheric foundation for nested containers, card tiers, and technical code blocks.

### Application Rules
- Maintain an expansive white/near-white ground (`#FFFFFF` to `#FCFCFC`). Do not saturate wide areas in lime.
- Acid lime (`#BAF534`) functions as an electrical signal: use it for small badges, status dots, metric deltas, and sharp micro-interactions against dark ink backdrops.
- Surface transitions step smoothly from `#FFFFFF` (canvas base) to `#F6F6F4` (primary container) to `#EEEEEA` (nested metadata or code block), bound by hairline outlines of `#E4E4E0`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is an interlocking triad:
1. **Headlines (`Bricolage Grotesque`):** Character-driven, compact, and confident. Provides modern editorial posture with high legibility at tight tracking.
2. **Body & Prose (`Inter`):** Neutral, transparent, and balanced. Maximizes reading stamina across documentation, configuration panels, and complex agent logs.
3. **Telemetry & Technical Metrics (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospaced precision for badges, status codes, logs, timestamps, parameters, and key data readouts.

### Typesetting Guidelines
- All uppercase metadata, labels, and badges must use `JetBrains Mono` with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`).
- Numerical figures within tabular data and agent performance indices must enforce tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- Avoid center-aligning body copy; maintain a strict left-ragged baseline alignment to reinforce the architectural, documentarian layout.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system pinned inside a max-width container of `1360px`.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `3rem` (`48px`) outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer canvas margins.

### Spacing Principles
- Layout adheres to a base 4px spatial increment, heavily weighted towards generous macro-spacing (`space-xl`, `space-2xl`) between logical thematic sections, contrasted by compact, dense micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) inside technical telemetry tables and code panels.
- Content reflow transitions from multi-column split panes on desktop directly into single-column vertical stacks on mobile, preserving strict left-border alignments.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy diffuse dropshadows in favor of **structural planar elevation** and **low-contrast micro-outlines**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Pure background tone (`#FFFFFF` or `#FCFCFC`).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Tonal card substrate (`#F6F6F4`) framed by a 1px solid hairline border in `#E4E4E0`. Zero blur, flat elevation.
- **Layer 2 (Nested Code Panels & Metrics):** Recessed or elevated inner module in `#EEEEEA` or `#FFFFFF` bounded by `#E4E4E0`.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Popovers, Menus, Modals):** White background (`#FFFFFF`), a crisp 1px `#111A16` structural border, and a subtle offset shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(7, 17, 14, 0.06), 0px 1px 3px rgba(7, 17, 14, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive Focus / Active Glow:** When elements gain focus or trigger execution states, employ a 1px border shift to `#07110E` augmented by a razor-thin 2px solid ring in `#BAF534`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** shape classification (`roundedness: 1`), optimizing for a crisp, engineered, lab-grade aesthetic:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius. Maintains structural tightness without the harshness of raw 0px corners.
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Full Modals & Sheet Surfaces:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) corner radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pills / Status Chips:** Specifically constrained to `9999px` only when presenting live agent states (e.g., `RUNNING`, `STANDBY`) to create clear differentiation from actionable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Dark ink fill (`#07110E`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` radius, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. On hover: subtle scale elevation with a 1px outline of `#BAF534` and interior text shifting to `#BAF534`.
- **Secondary / Technical:** Warm paper surface (`#F6F6F4`), 1px solid `#E4E4E0` border, `#07110E` typography. On hover: border transitions to `#07110E`, surface to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Accent (Action Trigger):** Electric lime background (`#BAF534`), black ink text (`#07110E`), bold weight (`600`). On hover: background shifts to `#BEF264` with a crisp hairline stroke in `#84CC16`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Monospace Agent Badge:** Uppercase JetBrains Mono text (`11px`), `4px` padding vertical, `8px` horizontal.
- **Active / Operational:** Subdued lime tint background (`rgba(186, 245, 52, 0.18)`), dark green text (`#111A16`), 1px border in `#84CC16`. Features a pulsating 6px solid `#BAF534` circular dot.
- **Neutral / Version Tag:** `#EEEEEA` surface, `#57605A` text, 1px `#E4E4E0` border.

### Cards & Content Containers
- Constructed with `#F6F6F4` background and a 1px solid `#E4E4E0` border.
- Cards maintain distinct internal header zones separated by a continuous horizontal hairline stroke (`#E4E4E0`).
- Interactive cards transition border color to `#111A16` on hover with a smooth 150ms ease.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px hairline border in `#E4E4E0`, `4px` radius, typography set to `Inter 14px` or `JetBrains Mono 13px` for parameters.
- **Focus State:** 1px border `#07110E` with an outer ring of `2px solid #BAF534`. No diffuse shadows.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square/circular controls with 1.5px `#111A16` borders. Checked states fill solid `#07110E` with an electric lime (`#BAF534`) check indicator.

### Lists & Telemetry Feeds
- Compact vertical rows divided by 1px horizontal `#E4E4E0` rules.
- Alternate zebra striping using `#FFFFFF` and `#F6F6F4`.
- Metadata cells right-align with fixed-width tabular numbers rendered in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Code Blocks & Terminal Windows
- Outer shell framed in `#F4F1EA` with an inner workspace of `#111A16` or pure `#FFFFFF` depending on density context.
- Header bar includes title tab and monospaced copy/execution actions.
